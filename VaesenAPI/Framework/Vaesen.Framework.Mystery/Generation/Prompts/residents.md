# Step: residents

Add three to five ordinary residents of the place who are not part of the case: a baker, a sexton, a stable hand, the inn's gossips. They make the place feel lived in and give the players someone to ask.

- Keep each short: a name, plotRole (one line: who they are here), importance resident, instanceRef new, an identity (age, occupation, rank, home, one line of appearance), homePlaceId (and homeAffordanceId where the level lists a dwelling for them), a routine, one line of voiceNotes, two opinions as drives, and one or two latent hooks as secrets. Leave guilt, permitted actions and relationships short or empty.
- They know nothing of the case beyond common talk.
- Swedish names of 1872: country people use patronymics. A son takes his father's first name and -son (Erik Ersson's son is Vilho Eriksson), a daughter -dotter (Karin Eriksdotter), and a wife keeps her own patronymic from her father, never her husband's (Erik's wife is Kaisa Jonsdotter). Family names (Lindqvist, Wester) belong to the gentry, townsfolk, soldiers and some families, and are inherited. Record a parent and child in relationships, so the name check knows they are kin.
- Names must be easy to tell apart by ear from everyone already in the case and from each other: no shared first name or first syllable. Already in the case: {{names}}.
