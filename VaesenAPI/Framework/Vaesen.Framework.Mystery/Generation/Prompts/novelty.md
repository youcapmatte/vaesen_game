# Read this plot into its signature

Read the plot below and choose one value for each axis, the one that fits it best. Judge the plot, not its wording.

- provocation: what set the creature off. trespass (someone entered its place or crossed its bounds), takenFromIt (something of its was taken or kept), harmToItsPlace (its place was felled, dammed, drained, built on or burned), brokenPromise (a promise or custom kept with it was broken), bargainSought (someone went to it for a bargain), bargainComesDue (an old bargain is now called in), desire (it wants a person for itself), revenge (a death or wrong done to it or its kin), insult (mockery, a curse, disrespect), neglect (offerings or old ways left off), summoned (a rite called it), hunger (it acts by its nature, unprovoked), other.
- bond: what ties people to it. none, bargain, debt, gift (luck or skill it gave), lover, child (a child of the creature, or a changeling), oath, curse, holding (someone keeps a thing of its), other.
- victim: who suffers most. child, youngWoman, youngMan, worker, household, livestock, community, bargainer (the one who dealt with it), outsider, other.
- cure: what the deep ending does. restore (give back what was taken), honour (keep the bargain or custom), release (break or buy out the bond), rite (an offering or old rite), truth (a confession or the truth told before others), church (baptism, a priest, consecrated ground), ward (steel, fire, salt), departure (someone leaves for good), sacrifice, other.
- oneLine: the plot in one line with no names: what the creature does, to whom, and why.
