# Seed the Society's world

This Society is about to play its first mystery. Before it does, write the people of its world: the ones it will meet again and again across many mysteries, who remember what the Society did and whose attitudes change with it.

The canonical characters above already exist in every Society's world; this Society has its own copy of each. Now add this Society's own people.

## Core cast: {{coreCount}} people

Important to the Society from the start, each with drives, a voice, ties and an attitude toward the Society.

- Spread them. Some belong to Uppsala and the Society's daily life near its headquarters: neighbours, suppliers, officials, rivals, family of the investigators where it fits. Others are contacts in the provinces, who can later send a letter, open a door or bar one.
- Vary rank, age, sex, temperament and attitude, from -3 (hostile) to +3 (devoted). At least two are sceptical or hostile, and at least one has a hidden agenda of their own. Nobody is a stereotype.
- Tie them to each other and to the canonical characters with relationships (ids from this list and the canonical ones) and network links (institution ids: {{institutions}}).
- Give each two to four latent hooks: a habit, debt, connection, secret or ambition the engine can later turn into a plot.

## Background cast: {{backgroundCount}} people

Named people with no story yet: a line of identity, a voice register and two or three latent hooks each, and no more. They live in Uppsala and in the provinces.

## Conventions

- Ids: npc-firstname-lastname, lowercase with hyphens, unique, never a canonical id.
- home: a town or a parish ("Uppsala", "Gävle", "a croft outside Sala").
- importance: 2 for core, 1 for background.
- Write originally from the history and daily life of the period; never copy any published game text.
- Answer with the JSON the schema asks for and nothing else.
