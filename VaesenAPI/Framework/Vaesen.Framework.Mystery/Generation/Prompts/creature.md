# Step: the creature's mind

The runtime plays the creature from this record alone, as it plays a person: write what it is, not what happens.

- {{creatureMindRule}}
- Its terms are the deep lever's deeds as the creature sees them: what it would accept, in its own terms. termDeedIds names those deeds.
- Its red lines are what turns a bargain into a war: steel in its domain, a broken promise, a lie it sees through.
- What it knows are fact ids of this case, as it witnessed them. What it thinks may be wrong about the people.
- Its reactions are short: what it does or says when the players try each approach, in a sentence or two.
