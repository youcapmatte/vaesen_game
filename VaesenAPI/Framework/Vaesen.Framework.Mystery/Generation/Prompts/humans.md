# Step: humans

Cast the people entangled in this conflict, then the location's ordinary residents.

- Derive roles and moral colour from the conflict and fill the archetype's role slots (roleSlotId). Vary them deliberately: the one who provoked may be sympathetic, the victim may be complicit, the warner may be wrong about why.
- Draw on the persistent characters offered first, if any of them plausibly belongs here (instanceRef persistent, keep their id; identity may be null for them). They keep their personality, voice, attitude toward the Society and what they remember; act on that history. A background character raised into this story is instanceRef promoted, with their id. Otherwise create new people (instanceRef new, with a full identity). Characters who are dead or gone are not offered and must not appear.
- importance in this case is only significant or resident. It is not the character's tier in the Society's world (core, background): a persistent core character who matters here is significant.
- Persistent characters may act from a distance through their network links (a hostile official closing doors, a grateful one opening them), even if they do not appear.
- Cast to embody the creature's deep-lore motifs where it fits (for a fiddling water spirit: a fiddler who became a master overnight).

- If the hidden hand holds power over someone through a threat, say in that victim's fears why they think the threat is real: their rank, the hand's standing, the lack of proof, or what the victim stands to lose that the hand does not. When exposure would cut both ways, say why the victim still believes they would lose.
- The hook-giver ({{hookGiverType}}) is a cast member with their own motive, possibly self-interested; their account will be testimony, not truth.
- Give everyone significant drives, fears, guilt where it applies, how exposed they believe they are (0-1), and the actions their drives permit as pressure rises (low: lie, deflect; medium: hide or destroy evidence, intimidate, plead; high: flee, confess, help the group, or at the extreme silence someone).
- Add three to five residents (importance resident) unconnected to the case: a baker, a priest, a stable hand, inn gossips, with routines and opinions.
- Five to seven significant characters is right: the case is played by voice, and listeners cannot hold more. Home places are place ids of the level. Where the level lists a dwelling for someone (a cottage, the barracks, a farmhouse kitchen), give it as homeAffordanceId. A significant person whose home place has nothing for them gets newDwelling true, and the engine builds them a home there; never put them in someone else's house or a shared building they do not live in.
- Names must be easy to tell apart by ear, for every named person in the case: the cast, the dead, and anyone the truth or the events only mention. No two share a first name or a first syllable, and no surname contains another's first name (not Johan and Jonas, not Erik and Elin, not Per and Persson). Give every significant character a short handle (handle) players can use instead of the name ("the widow", "the wise man", "the buyer").
