# Step: hook and scenes

Write how the Society is drawn in, and the places the case is played in.

Hook
- The hook arrives by {{hookChannel}} from the hook-giver (hookGiverId, a cast member). It carries just enough convergence to justify the journey: it reports opening events (openingEventIds) and states facts (assertedFactIds), never the cause. Use a detail that does not fit, a strained official explanation, the teller's own unease, stakes and urgency. Everything it states is a fact like any other.
- {{giveawayRule}}
- The hook-giver is {{giverRule}}, and the one person who brought the Society: if the people's records say someone else did, the hook is theirs, or theirs must change.
- The hook-giver writes only what they know (their knows), and names people as the case names them: a relative of theirs is a cast member or one of the offstage people, by that name.
- If the hook says how to come, or how long the message took, use only the routes the level lists for this month (routesThisMonth). Name only the towns and authorities the level's geography lists.

Scenes
- List the places the case uses (placesUsed): every place a channel, a beat or the climax uses. Write a scene description for each, in this month's weather and light. The game master reads it aloud when the players arrive, so it shows only what anyone sees then: the mood, the people about, and the traces whose channels need nothing. Never what a channel keeps behind trust, a roll, a time, an item or another fact: no hidden object, no stained bedclothes, no wound, not even half-seen.
